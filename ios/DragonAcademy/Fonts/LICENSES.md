The seven families the font themes use (src/data/fontThemes.js) are all from
[github.com/google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts), under `ofl/`, and
all are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1** (OFL-1.1).
Checked against each family's `METADATA.pb` (`license: "OFL"`) and `OFL.txt`
in that repo (commit 296bb4790d3f9bd95547c733708a3666ed35d844, 2026-09-25).

The OFL lets us bundle and redistribute the fonts inside the app, free of
charge, provided each copy keeps the copyright notice and this license
(condition 2) and the fonts are not sold on their own (condition 1). We ship
the files unmodified. A **Reserved Font Name** (Quicksand) only matters for a
*modified* version, which must then be renamed (condition 3) — so never subset
or edit the files; replace them with newer upstream files instead.
