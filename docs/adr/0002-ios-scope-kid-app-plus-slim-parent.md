# iOS app is the kid app plus a slim parent view

The iOS app includes every kid learning feature (map, battles, trial, collection, Learning Lair, Proving Grounds, spelling, phonics, memorize, settings, and guest mode), plus a slim parent view. The parent view covers adding children, kid QR codes, basic stats, purchases, account deletion, the contact email and practice reminders. Parent dashboards, custom spelling lists, billing management, and the teacher, school and admin pages stay web-only, since none of them benefit from being native. The web kid screens keep running for Chromebook and Android users.

Tribes (friends, profiles, leaderboards) is deliberately left out of version 1. It puts user-generated profiles in a kids' app, which requires reporting and blocking under App Review guideline 1.2, and the product works without it.
